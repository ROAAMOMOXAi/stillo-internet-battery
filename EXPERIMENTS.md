# Experiments

## Experiment 001: Charge and Redeem

Compare two runs using the same connectivity scenario.

### Baseline
Connectivity disappears. No persistent resource is created.

### STILLO
Connectivity is observed, a resource is charged and persisted, connectivity disappears, a later connection occurs, and the resource is redeemed.

### Required measurements

- available capacity
- contact duration
- transferred data
- charged amount
- persisted amount
- redeemed amount
- expired/lost amount
- outage duration
- redemption delay
- service delivered
- baseline service
- STILLO service
- overhead
- efficiency

Results must be generated by the experiment, not invented.
